Privacy

Short, like the menu will be honest

What this site collects

This site has no advertising trackers, no analytics scripts and no cookies. The only information it takes is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, an optional phone number, your email and your message.

Where it goes

The contact form delivers your message directly to Brick & Basil’s inbox so the shop can answer you — for opening questions, catering bookings and event inquiries. It isn’t sold, rented or added to any third-party list.

Hosting and mail delivery

The site is static HTML hosted on Railway, and form messages are relayed by Brevo, an email-delivery provider, purely to reach the shop’s inbox. Standard web-request logs (IP address, browser type, requested page) exist for uptime and abuse checks and aren’t used for profiling.
