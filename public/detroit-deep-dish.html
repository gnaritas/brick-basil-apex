02 · The Pie The Truck Was Built On

Detroit-style deep dish, the way the truck made it

The crispy golden edges. The buttery crust. The layers of cheese and sauce. This is the pizza the Rolling Deep food truck specialized in — the reason the truck was a fixture around Wake County, now with a dining room under it.

Why Detroit-style

Chef Scott Harvey’s truck specialized in deep dish pizza — the perceived gap in the market for a properly-made scratch pie is exactly what pushed the truck toward a permanent home at The Ballast on Salem.

  • Baked in a rectangular pan, not round — the edges get crispy and golden
  • Layers of cheese and sauce, built the Detroit way
  • Dough made in the glass pasta room next door
  • The pizza Rolling Deep specialized in — served at a table
