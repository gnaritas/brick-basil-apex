Our Story

From food truck roots to a forever home

Brick & Basil is an authentic Italian concept with food-truck roots — the next chapter of a kitchen that has been building toward a dining room for years.

  1. Raised In It

    A restaurant childhood, upstate New York

    Scott Harvey grew up in the restaurant business — his father was a chef. He worked high-end kitchens in upstate New York before moving into restaurant consulting, helping other owners open rooms, fix operations and plan buildouts.

  2. The Truck Era

    Harvey Hospitality Catering + Rolling Deep

    Around four years ago he started his own catering company and launched Rolling Deep, a food truck built on Detroit-style deep dish pizzas and fusion egg rolls. The truck’s success — and a gap he saw in the market for high-quality deep dish pizza — pointed toward four walls.

  3. Now

    Brick & Basil, at The Ballast on Salem

    Plans for Brick & Basil are filed with the city of Apex and the buildout is next: 50 seats inside, 35 on a new patio, and a glass-enclosed pasta room at the front of the house. Opening early 2027. The truck is on hiatus while the forever home comes together — catering stays open for business.

“My grandfather was a butcher, and I didn’t even see a grocery store till I was 16.”

Scott Harvey, on the farm childhood behind the kitchen’s sourcing rules

The rules of the house

Clean food, trusted sourcing, everything made from scratch without preservatives. Most ingredients imported from Italy; the rest from North Carolina organic products and area farmers — organic-milled flour, mozzarella stretched in house, filtered tap water on the table.
