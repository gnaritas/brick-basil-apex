03 · Off The Same Dough

Focaccia sandwiches & the rest of the table

Scratch-made and preservative-free is the rule for everything this kitchen turns out — the pasta, the pizza dough and the focaccia alike. Sandwiches and other Italian dishes come off that same standard.

A

Focaccia sandwiches

Focaccia sandwiches are on the menu from day one. Fillings and names land with the opening announcement — what’s certain is that they’re made from scratch.

B

Other Italian plates

The menu goes beyond pasta, pie and sandwiches into other Italian dishes — the full lineup is coming with opening. The kitchen’s rules of the house don’t change: imported Italian staples, organic North Carolina produce and area farms, nothing with a preservative list.
