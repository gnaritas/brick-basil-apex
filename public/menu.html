The Menu

Written the way the kitchen works

Everything starts from scratch — organic-milled flour, mozzarella stretched on site, dough made in the glass pasta room. Most ingredients are imported from Italy; the rest come from North Carolina organic farms and area growers. Menu items and prices will be announced as the opening gets closer.

01

Handmade pasta

A chef-driven pasta program — noodles rolled and cut by hand in view of the dining room. Gluten-free options are part of the plan at opening.

02

Detroit-style deep dish

The square pan, the crispy cheese edges, the layers. This is the food the Rolling Deep truck was known for — now with dining-room tables under it.

03

Focaccia sandwiches & other Italian plates

Focaccia sandwiches and other Italian dishes round out the menu — the full lineup is coming with the opening announcement.
