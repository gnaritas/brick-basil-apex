From the truck to a forever home

Chef Scott Harvey grew up in the restaurant business, worked upstate New York kitchens and restaurant consulting, then built Rolling Deep — the Detroit-style deep dish and fusion egg roll truck. Brick & Basil is its forever home: the truck is on hiatus while the buildout happens, and the catering kitchen keeps booking.
