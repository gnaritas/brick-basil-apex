Image from Brick & Basil APEX, NC – Brick & Basil, a new Italian restaurant from chef and caterer Scott Harvey, is targeting an April 2027 opening at 1801 Chatham Flts Dr #4 Apex, NC 27502 , Harvey told Business Debut in a phone interview. What to Expect Brick & Basil will be an authentic Italian concept with a chef-driven menu centered on fresh, handmade pasta, deep dish pizza, focaccia sandwiches and other Italian dishes. Chef Scott Harvey will import most ingredients from Italy, supplementing with local organic products and working with area farmers. The team will stretch mozzarella on site. Harvey shared with Business Debut that even the flour will be organically milled and that he intends to provide gluten-free options and filtered tap water for patrons. The restaurant will feature about 50 seats inside and another 35 on a newly built patio, Harvey shared with Business Debut in a phone interview. Guests will see a pasta room enclosed in glass where staff will prepare pasta, pizza dough and other items in view. Harvey said the space is designed with state-of-the-art equipment to handle significant volume. The service model will offer flexibility. Guests can seat themselves and order from their phones using QR codes or opt for full service with a traditional server, Harvey told Business Debut. The food truck that preceded the restaurant, Rolling Deep, is on hiatus during the buildout while Harvey focuses on the opening and his catering work, he said. More progress and the outdoor area. Images from Brick & Basil Ownership Background Chef Harvey grew up in the restaurant business. His father was a chef, and Harvey worked in high-end kitchens in upstate New York before moving into restaurant consulting. As a consultant, he helped clients open restaurants, resolve operational problems and plan buildouts. About four years ago, Harvey started his own catering company, Harvey Hospitality Catering, and launched the Rolling Deep food truck, which specialized in deep dish pizzas and fusion egg rolls. The truck’s success and a perceived gap in the market for high-quality deep dish pizza and scratch-made food led him toward a permanent space. Harvey traced his approach to ingredients back to his childhood on a farm in upstate New York. “My grandfather was a butcher, and I didn’t even see a grocery store till I was 16,” he said. His background informs Harvey's focus on clean food, trusted sourcing and making items from scratch without preservatives. Opening Timeline Harvey said he has submitted plans to the city and is waiting for approval so construction can begin. “Our target date’s April 2027,” he told Business Debut. He expects to start the buildout within the next few weeks. Follow for More Harvey said Facebook is the main source of updates while the Brick & Basil website is in development.
